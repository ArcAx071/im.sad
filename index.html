A little something from me to you

I'm Sorry.

I know that simply saying “sorry” can't magically fix everything, but I still want you to know how genuinely sorry I am.

I’m sorry for the things I did that hurt you, for the moments when I could have understood you better, and for anything I made you feel that you didn't deserve.

I don't want to make excuses or turn this into a list of reasons. I just want to take responsibility and tell you that your feelings matter to me.

You mean a lot to me, and I hate knowing that my actions became a reason for you to feel hurt or disappointed. If I could go back, there are things I would do differently.

I can't promise I'll never make mistakes again. I can promise that I'll try harder to learn from them, listen better, and treat your heart with the care it deserves.
Made with a little courage, a lot of regret, and genuine love.
